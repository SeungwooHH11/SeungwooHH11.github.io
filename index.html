💡  Research Interests
Focus: Simulation-based modeling and optimization of complex engineering systems.
Methods: Scheduling and routing optimization, graph neural networks, and reinforcement learning.


I received my M.S. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering from Seoul National University (SNU), where I worked in the Production Engineering Laboratory (PEL) under the supervision of Prof. Jong Hun Woo.

My research focuses on simulation-based modeling and optimization of complex engineering systems, particularly scheduling and routing problems in industrial logistics and maritime autonomous systems. I use graph neural networks and reinforcement learning as methodological tools for learning-based sequential decision-making in real-world engineering systems.

My previous and ongoing work includes shipyard block transportation scheduling, hull-block placement and retrieval, explainable AI for scheduling, and multi-USV rendezvous scheduling. If you are interested in discussing research or potential collaboration, please feel free to contact me via email.
